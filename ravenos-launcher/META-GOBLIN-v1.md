## Privacy law

Shade Sense deliberately strips package, channel, title, body, extras, and notification keys from the dialogue-facing structural snapshot. It may expose category, importance, conversation flag, ambient/filter state, plausible alerting, burst pressure, removal payoff, and locally measured lifetime.

NotificationSenseOS may still retain the owner's selected local SOURCE / SEMANTIC / FULL_LOCAL depth for local evidence surfaces. That does not increase the Meta Goblin dialogue layer's authority.

## Raven settlement

The whole office stays present. The office stops narrating Android like a sports announcer.

Visible commentary follows these laws:

1. **OFFICE PRESENCE != OFFICE CHATTER** — all canonical office members remain in the cast; silence does not remove them.
2. **PHONE ACTION != COMMENT-WORTHY MOMENT** — app switches, windows, media edges, and notification callbacks may update evidence without producing visible speech.
3. **SCREEN MEANING > APP EDGE** — fresh owner-authorized visible semantics/OCR outrank foreground/package activity when composing a line.
4. **EVIDENCE MAY UPDATE SILENTLY** — MarkerBus, scene state, callbacks, recurrence, shade geometry, and receipts continue even when the resident says nothing.
5. **READ THE GLASS OR ADMIT UNKNOWN** — when Accessibility/Goblin Read has fresh bounded context, commentary names what is actually visible. When protected/unavailable, it does not invent screen meaning.
6. **META EARNS META** — if the visible screen itself mentions RavenOS, the office, Goblin Vision, or related self-referential material, the commentary may explicitly notice the fourth-wall event.
7. **ONE PRIMARY VOICE** — one owner speaks per earned moment. A second office aside is reserved for rare screen-proven meta recursion.
8. **COMEDY IS DOWNSTREAM OF CONTEXT** — the original Mayhem bank may add one short owner-native stinger only after a screen-aware truth or a material boundary/error/payoff.
9. **SENSING != AUTHORITY** — all commentary remains local presentation; `effectAuthority = NONE`.

## ScreenContextOS

`RavenScreenContextOS` is a composition organ, not a new sensor or permission surface. It consumes only already-authorized local facts:

- `RavenAccessibilityReadOS` — visible semantics with password/editable-value boundaries;
- `RavenGoblinReadOS` — owner-armed local OCR with sensitive-screen suppression;
- `RavenScreenMapOS` — normalized OCR geometry;
- `RavenPhoneSceneOS` — active application context.

Fresh Accessibility semantics are preferred, with local OCR as fallback. The organ derives a bounded focus phrase, app semantic class, meta-recursion flag, layout zones, and a screen-content novelty signature. It does not retain raw frames.

## Cadence

Normal spoken minimum gaps are deliberately human-scale instead of callback-scale:

| Haunt mode | Normal speech floor |
| --- | ---: |
| CALM | 75 s |
| LIVED_IN | 45 s |
| HAUNTED | 32 s |
| FERAL | 22 s |
| APOCALYPSE | 14 s |

Material boundary/error/payoff/very-high-salience events may break the normal floor after a smaller bounded critical gap.

Screen novelty is remembered longer than an Android callback. Repeated reads of the same visible subject normally remain silent for roughly 1–5 minutes depending on haunt mode.

## Intended behavioral difference

Old style:

```text
App changed.
Window changed.
Music continued.
Notification arrived.
Another app changed.
```

Screen-first style:

```text
💗 KYU
ChatGPT is actually on “their statements are too rapid, too unaware of the actual context of the screen” right now.

BONK. Yes. That is the bug report on the glass.
```

Then the office shuts up until the visible subject materially changes or another moment actually earns commentary.
